# adieu-app

![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)
![Status: early](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-early-orange.svg)

Adieu finds the online accounts you've forgotten about, and helps you close the ones you don't want anymore — without any of your data ever leaving your computer.

> "Dites adieu à vos vieux comptes."

## What it is

This is the free part of Adieu: a web app that runs entirely in your browser. You give it an export of your mailbox or password manager, and it:

- finds the online accounts hidden in there
- shows you how old each one is, and flags the ones that might still be costing you money
- lets you decide, account by account, what to keep and what to delete
- gives you a direct link to delete it, or a ready-to-send deletion request

Nothing is ever uploaded: it works fully offline, and you can download it to run with no internet connection at all.

Adieu also wants to offer a paid service that sends and chases those deletion requests for you — that part is a separate component, not this one, and it will get built only if enough people are interested.

## Status

Early, pre-release. This is a side project exploring whether it's worth building further.
